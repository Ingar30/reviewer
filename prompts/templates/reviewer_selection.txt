Classification guidance:
- empirical_causal: empirical paper making causal, treatment-effect, identification, quasi-experimental, experimental, or policy-effect claims.
- empirical_descriptive: empirical paper mainly describing patterns, correlations, measurement, forecasting, or facts without causal identification as the main contribution.
- theory: theoretical, conceptual, or model-driven paper with little or no empirical analysis.
- methods: paper introducing an estimator, measurement method, dataset construction method, or computational procedure.
- literature_review: survey or literature review paper.
- mixed: multiple major paper types are central.
- unknown: parsed artifacts are too limited to classify confidently.

Selection rules:
- Treat reviewer selection as a quality-first coverage decision, not a checklist. A typical substantive paper should select 7 to 13 optional reviewers, and never more than 13. Prefer a focused roster, but use the upper end for long or methodologically heterogeneous papers and do not skip a reviewer with a distinct plausible high-impact issue class merely to reach the lower end of the range.
- For every selected optional reviewer, the reason must identify the concrete parsed cue and the distinct risk it will audit. If two reviewers would likely inspect the same claims, select the more direct reviewer and skip the other with a reason. If two reviewers inspect the same manuscript passage through materially different lenses, such as identification versus model notation or sample construction versus institutional data context, they are not redundant.
- Select numerical_auditor for papers with important numeric claims, tables, figures, or appendix estimates.
- Select claim_evidence_auditor for papers with substantive empirical, policy, or interpretive claims tied to displayed evidence.
- Select literature_auditor for papers with novelty, positioning, or prior-work characterization claims.
- Select identification_auditor for empirical_causal papers or mixed papers with causal/identification claims. Also select it for methods or descriptive papers that make causal mechanism, decomposition, convergent-validity, internal-validity, or directional behavioral claims that require identification assumptions beyond general claim-evidence alignment.
- Select robustness_auditor for empirical papers with robustness checks, alternative specifications, subsamples, placebo tests, or appendix validations.
- Select sample_construction_auditor for empirical papers with nontrivial sample restrictions, attrition, variable construction, exclusions, or denominators.
- Select abstract_conclusion_consistency_auditor when abstract/introduction/conclusion claims appear important enough to check against the body.
- Select limitations_external_validity_auditor for papers making policy, welfare, generalizability, or scope claims.
- Select model_equation_auditor for theory, structural, model-heavy, equation-heavy, or methods papers. For a methods paper with regression estimands, generated constructs, transformed outcomes, or code/variable definitions, treat this audit as distinct from identification, numerical, and robustness review. Also select it for empirical papers where equations, instruments, generated variables, fixed effects, clustering, estimands, or model notation are central enough that an equation/estimand audit is likely to catch non-overlapping problems.
- Select data_availability_replication_auditor only for experiments, RCTs, preregistered or pre-planned empirical designs, explicit pre-analysis-plan claims, registry links or IDs, concrete replication/reproducibility claims, or data/code availability claims that materially affect the paper's credibility. Treat mentions of platforms such as AEA RCT Registry, OSF Registries, AsPredicted, and similar services as strong selection cues.
- Select institutional_context_auditor when material legal, regulatory, platform, market-design, institutional-process, voting-rule, event-context, historical-institutional, confidential/admin-data, registry, archive, or institutional-record claims affect interpretation and targeted external verification would add value beyond literature/reference checks. Explicit cues include claims about API training, storage, retention, privacy, or data controls and dated descriptions of tariffs, laws, elections, platform rules, or policy implementation. Do not treat institutional context as redundant with sample construction when the paper's data sources, record systems, sample frame, or historical institutions could create distinct validity risks.
- Select power_multiple_testing_auditor when empirical results involve preregistrations, many outcomes, many subgroup tests, many treatment arms, selected outcome-code pairs, equality or equivalence language, strong null-effect claims, or unclear confirmatory versus exploratory status. Select it for a many-outcome validation family even when no preregistration is disclosed: the absence of a declared confirmatory family increases rather than removes the need for this audit. Numerical and robustness review are not substitutes for multiplicity, selection, equivalence, or power analysis.
- Select design_randomization_auditor whenever a manuscript reports and interprets a substantive experiment, RCT, survey experiment, randomized offer, or assigned-treatment module. Its audit includes treatment assignment, omitted arms, attrition after assignment, spillovers, contamination, carryover, implementation fidelity, hypothetical choices, and stated-versus-revealed behavior. An experiment used to validate a paper's method or behavioral claim is a material cue even when it is a secondary module. Identification and robustness review are not substitutes for assignment and implementation review. Do not select it for ordinary observational comparisons or a random display order that supports no substantive result.
- Select economic_magnitude_auditor when the paper makes central welfare, willingness-to-pay, cost, scalability, calibration, investment-share, policy-magnitude, practical-significance, relative-versus-absolute percentage, or "economically large" claims where a separate scale and denominator audit could catch non-overlapping errors.

Pilot-reviewer budget:
- The pilot reviewers are data_availability_replication_auditor, institutional_context_auditor, power_multiple_testing_auditor, design_randomization_auditor, and economic_magnitude_auditor.
- Select up to 4 pilot reviewers, and only when each has a distinct, central cue and plausible material issue class.

When unsure, prefer selecting a reviewer if its audit would catch high-impact errors and the parsed artifacts contain enough material to inspect. Quality takes priority over saving one reviewer call. For pilot reviewers, require a clear selection cue and a plausible material issue class; do not select them merely because they could make generic documentation or presentation suggestions. Use skipped_optional_reviewers for every reviewer not selected: every optional reviewer in the catalog must appear exactly once in either selected_optional_reviewers or skipped_optional_reviewers.
